# PGx CPIC and FDA Evidence

CPIC and FDA evidence is stored separately from CMS/payer evidence with source identifier, version, publication/effective dates, hash, review status, and supersession.

- CPIC explains how available PGx results may inform therapy; it does not decide whether testing should occur or establish Medicare support.
- FDA pharmacogenetic associations and labels do not independently establish payer support.
- Neither source may be converted into unsupported treatment advice.

Sources:

- https://cpicpgx.org/guidelines/
- https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/precision-medicine/table-pharmacogenetic-associations

Automated CPIC/FDA version import remains a Phase 2 completion gate; the current reference seed is not sufficient for production evidence decisions.
